# Exit Handover

Read at every exit, before offering durable recording. Carry each item
with its appropriate home; the user may redirect or decline the offer.

- **Constituted design decisions:** preserve the user's words and constitutive basis.
  A decision redirecting PR work belongs with the issues that PR declares it closes.
  For stacked repairs, follow the PR links to the work's issue references; a repair
  layer declares no closing issues itself. If no layer declares a home, say so.
- **Unsettled conflicts:** carry both governing surfaces, their disagreement, and
  the authority relation or its absence. Preserve the unsettled status.
- **Successor work:** provide each handed-over finding's location and verifying
  evidence as entry points into the work, with its scope/disposition basis.
- **Directions beyond this unit:** preserve the proposal and falsifier, marked
  unconstituted. This exit proposal does not enter the reviewer's design-intent ledger.

Show the proposed record and destination. Execute recording under the user's existing
or newly supplied authorization. Exit itself has already taken effect and remains
independent of that decision.
