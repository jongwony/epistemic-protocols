# Exit Handover

What reaches past this loop, and the durable home each kind implies. Nothing here bears on how a round is run or how a finding is dispositioned — it binds once, at the moment the loop ends, which is where it loads. Read it at exit, converged or free, before the offer is made.

What governs the exit itself stays in `SKILL.md`'s Convergence section and is deliberately not restated here: a second statement of a rule is a copy, and a copy drifts from what it copies.

## Ledger carryover

An entry enters the design-decision ledger (Phase 0 harvest, source 4) precisely because it holds independently of current code state — its reach already exceeds the diff that occasioned it. The ledger is nonetheless loop-local: an entry re-enters the source's bundle at the next re-harvest and otherwise ends with the session, so the admission criterion and the lifetime disagree. The entries that lose most are the ones that redirect the work the diff was serving rather than settling it — a direction the user constituted *against* the diff, which the diff itself does not record. With the loop gone that direction is unrecoverable, and the next session to pick the work up re-derives the frame the user just rejected.

At exit — converged or free — surface each ledger entry with the durable home its reach implies: for a PR scope, a decision redirecting the work belongs on the issues the PR declares it closes, where that work is next read. Where the loop stacked its repairs above the PR it reviewed, the layer it created is opened declaring no issues of its own — it records where a repair landed, not what the work was for, and a layer that declares them anyway sends the criterion to the top of the stack, which is the one home this reasoning rules out — so the criterion resolves down the stack, to whichever PR does declare them. That is the same criterion as ever rather than a second one written for stacks, which is why it is put this way and not as a rule about position: a stack whose layers all declare nothing has no home to pick, and the offer says that rather than choosing one by depth. Carry the user's constituting words verbatim rather than a summary, which keeps the conclusion and drops the frame that produced it — the frame is what the next reader needs. This is an offer, not a condition: the exit has already landed when the offer is made, declining ends the loop identically, and the user may redirect the record's shape or home.

## What travels beside the ledger

Other things reach past the loop for the ledger's own reason — their reach already exceeds the diff — while failing its admission criterion, that an entry be a decision already constituted. Each takes the same offer.

A **conflict left unsettled** travels as the conflict itself: each side with the surface it rests on, the point they disagree about, and the authority relation between them or the stated absence of one. Carrying a verdict nobody gave would be worse than carrying nothing, since the next reader cannot tell a recorded settlement from an assumed one.

The **far side of a boundary the user drew** travels as an entry point: each finding with where it holds and what establishes it, so whoever picks the work up starts from the finding instead of rediscovering it.

A **direction that exceeds this unit** — a reading, whether the source returned it or the loop formed it, proposing a change of shape this unit is not carrying — travels as the proposal together with the observation that would break it, marked as unconstituted. That last is not the design-decision ledger admitting a shared-cause reading by another door: the ledger's bar keeps such a reading out of every surface a later round's harvest reads, and this record is written at exit, after the last round, for a reader outside the loop. What it must not do is arrive looking settled, which is why it carries its falsifier and its unconstituted standing with it — a proposal recorded as a decision is worse than one not recorded at all.

That side is the run's deliverable rather than its remainder — a run can be complete and still hand most of what it found onward, and a record shaped as leftovers misreports the run to the person who has to act on it.
