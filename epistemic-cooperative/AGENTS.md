# `epistemic-cooperative/`

**Responsibility under the Northstar** (root `AGENTS.md`). The core protocols each live in their own directory; this plugin holds the utility skills that work around them — helping a person find the protocol that fits, checking and reviewing work at decision points of their own, and auditing the prose that steers the agent. It is a plugin of its own, not a protocol.

**Boundary.** Each `skills/<name>/SKILL.md` is that skill's own contract.
