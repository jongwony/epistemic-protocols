## Recipe: Decompose Recovery (object-level)

Decompose recovery repairs a **wrong fusion**: an abstraction formed over dissimilar instances forced under one form. The repair goes backward to the instances and re-forms, often into *multiple* abstractions. It factors into three object-level moves the existing algebra already arranges:

1. **Detect / evidence the split boundary** → `/ground` (Analogia) in its self-grounding case: ground the abstraction against its own instances, producing the **split-vs-trim partition reading**. Only a **Split** reading (**≥2 non-empty cells**) warrants decompose, and that ≥2-cell count satisfies the conduct warrant. A **Trim** reading (≤1 non-empty cell) is a single-move `/induce` (Narrow or Reorient), so the relay-test deactivates conduction.
2. **Constitute the cell assignment** → an in-session **Constitution checkpoint**: the candidate cells (core + rivals) come from `/ground`'s reading and **are the conducted moves at design time**; what the checkpoint defers and constitutes is the **membership refinement** — which instances land in which cell, and where each `trim_outlier` goes. Its pre-gate evidence is `/ground`'s partition reading — the rival-essence clusters, the `core_remainder`, and the `trim_outliers` — re-read from the upstream `/ground` output at the checkpoint (reference, not copy) and carried through session text. The checkpoint settles the membership of the core cell **plus** the rival cells, and decides each outlier (narrow it out, or place it in a cell). It leaves **every cell non-empty**: `/induce` requires at least one instance, so a cell the refinement empties is one the substrate does not dispatch. This is a substrate instruction carried in the checkpoint's brief, not a protocol transition — the checkpoint fires after handoff, so the conducted plan is no longer this protocol's to revise, and the design-time sub-two-move relay exit is unreachable from here. Where the refinement would empty **any** cell, the brief says so at the checkpoint, and re-conducting the reduced fan is a fresh `/conduct` the user starts — never a partial run of the frozen fan: `MoveSet` and `move_assignment` are fixed at handoff and a region completes only once every move assigned to it stops, so an undispatched cell's move leaves its region incomplete and blocks every frontier the plan conditions bind to it. Emptying all but one cell is the extreme of this case, not a separate one. Analogia *evidences* the candidate partition; this checkpoint *constitutes* the final membership.
3. **Re-form each cell** → a normal `/induce` (Periagoge) activation per cell, across the **whole** partition (each rival cell *and* the core cell).

**Topology instantiation** (the five axes, all existing Gen values). The three protocols are sequenced through session text (`/ground` detect → `/conduct` → `/induce` per cell); `/conduct` conducts the **induce-fan** — `/ground` is the upstream detection stage, *not* a conduct move-region. The conducted **`MoveSet` is concrete at design time**: it is `{ induce(cell) | cell ∈ the non-empty candidate cells }` over `/ground`'s **candidate partition**. A Split reading yields ≥2 non-empty candidate cells, so `|MoveSet| ≥ 2` satisfies the Phase 1 guard. The entry **cell-assignment checkpoint refines membership** of moves that already exist, never creating new moves after handoff. The axes resolve over the fan (uniform across its per-cell branches):
- **order** = `parallel_fan` over the per-cell `/induce` moves: independent, no ordering dependency among the cells. The fan is **uniform**, so the single region `whole` covers all moves. The cell-assignment checkpoint is the entry in-session checkpoint that gates the fan; the cell *count* comes from the candidate partition, the checkpoint settles the membership.
- **independence** = `isolated`: each cell branch re-forms independently — no cross-cell contamination before its abstraction crystallizes.
- **reconciliation** = `aggregate` over the branch outputs: the cells yield a **multiplicity** of `CrystallizedAbstraction`s — they are **not fused**.
- **termination** = `until_goal_met` per branch (each cell crystallizes); termination ground = `protocol_contract`: `/induce`'s own convergence contract (`CrystallizedAbstraction`) per branch.
- **routing** = `return_to_user`: each crystallized abstraction returns.
