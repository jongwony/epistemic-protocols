## Recipe: Decompose Recovery (object-level)

Decompose recovery repairs a **wrong fusion**: an abstraction formed over dissimilar instances forced under one form. The repair goes backward to the instances and re-forms, often into *several* abstractions. It factors into three object-level moves:

1. **Read the split** → `/ground` (Analogia) in its self-grounding case: ground the abstraction against its own instances. Its reading may be summarized as a split into rival groups, a trim of outliers, or a hold — a summary of the reading, not a verdict `/ground` computes. Where the reading is that the instances split into two or more rival groups, the per-group work is several lines whose conduct is worth a method; where it keeps one group, the repair is a single `/induce` (Narrow or Reorient).
2. **Settle the membership** → a deferred decision that returns to the person unless a recorded grant's reach covers it, shown as covered on the map: the candidate groups (core plus rivals) come from `/ground`'s reading and are the lines of work in the method; what is deferred is the membership — which instances land in which group, and where each outlier goes. Its evidence, shown where it returns or on the map as covered, is `/ground`'s reading — the rival clusters, the core remainder, and the outliers — re-read from the upstream output at that point (reference, not copy). `/induce` needs at least one instance, so a group the membership empties is one the substrate does not run. `/ground` evidences the candidate groups; this decision settles the membership.
3. **Re-form each group** → a normal `/induce` (Periagoge) activation per group, across the whole reading (each rival group *and* the core group).

**How the method usually reads** (a draft, not a template): the three protocols are sequenced through session text (`/ground` reads → `/conduct` → `/induce` per group); `/conduct` conducts the per-group `/induce` work, and `/ground` is upstream, not a line of the method. The groups typically run side by side, without seeing each other's results before they are combined, since each abstraction should crystallize from its own instances; their results are kept together as several `CrystallizedAbstraction`s rather than fused; each line stops when `/induce`'s own convergence contract is met; and the results go back to the person in the end summary. Where the work in front of you differs, draw the method from it.
