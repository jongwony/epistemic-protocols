# `horismos/` — `/bound`

**Responsibility under the Northstar** (root `AGENTS.md`). The protocol makes the work's goal, direction, and boundaries recognizable as a current map, including choices already made within grants and proposals for what remains open. The AI first lays out a whole provisional map of what the task rests on, with every open item filled in — its own choice inside a grant, or its proposal marked as such; the person opens whatever part they need, corrects the map, and decides what to keep, take, ask again, or entrust, to the depth they judge sufficient.

**Boundary.** `skills/bound/SKILL.md` is the contract for `/bound`; it names any supporting file in its `references/` to read where that file applies. `README.md` and `README_ko.md` explain the protocol to people, and `.claude-plugin/` and `.codex-plugin/` carry what a host needs to list and install it; what the protocol does once invoked is set in the `SKILL.md`.
