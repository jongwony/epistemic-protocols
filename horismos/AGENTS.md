# `horismos/` — `/bound`

**Responsibility under the Northstar** (root `AGENTS.md`). The protocol here makes the decisions inside a task visible before any of them is delegated. The AI first lays out a whole provisional map of the task's decisions, obligations, assumptions, and dependencies; the person opens whatever part they need, corrects the map, and decides what to keep, inspect, or entrust, to the depth they judge sufficient.

**Boundary.** `skills/bound/SKILL.md` is the contract for `/bound`, and the files in its `references/` are parts of that contract it points to where they apply. `README.md` and `README_ko.md` explain the protocol to people, and `.claude-plugin/` and `.codex-plugin/` carry what a host needs to list and install it; what the protocol does once invoked is set in the `SKILL.md`.
