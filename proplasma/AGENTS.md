# `proplasma/` — `/preview`

**Responsibility under the Northstar** (root `AGENTS.md`). The protocol here lets the person commit to a direction they could not judge from descriptions. Right before that commitment, cheap throwaway probes are built that differ on the axes where the candidates really diverge, so the decision is made on futures the person can see; the probes are discarded and never treated as evidence.

**Boundary.** `skills/preview/SKILL.md` is the contract for `/preview`, and the files in its `references/` are parts of that contract it points to where they apply. `README.md` and `README_ko.md` explain the protocol to people, and `.claude-plugin/` and `.codex-plugin/` carry what a host needs to list and install it; what the protocol does once invoked is set in the `SKILL.md`.
