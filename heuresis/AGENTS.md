# `heuresis/` — `/ideate`

**Responsibility under the Northstar** (root `AGENTS.md`). The protocol here widens a field of ideas that is empty or narrowed too early, before anything is chosen. Generation here never ranks, scores, or discards, so choosing stays a separate act that belongs to the person, and the field is not quietly shaped by what the AI would have picked.

**Boundary.** `skills/ideate/SKILL.md` is the contract for `/ideate`, and the files in its `references/` are parts of that contract it points to where they apply. `README.md` and `README_ko.md` explain the protocol to people, and `.claude-plugin/` and `.codex-plugin/` carry what a host needs to list and install it; what the protocol does once invoked is set in the `SKILL.md`.
