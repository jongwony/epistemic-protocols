# Chain reference — post-classification law for named material

Read when the request names material — a prior protocol's output in the session, or a record
outside it read by a tool — once that material has folded in, before the first pass; and when
the request resumes an earlier field, before its map is redrawn. This reference does not bind
on `Entry = Seeded` generally — only when the entry carries named material.

Named material folds in as seeds and is promoted to candidates on the first pass exactly like
the user's own fragments, gaining the frame each lands under. Each seed keeps the origin tag
the material already carries, or otherwise the origin of the turn it was read from —
`external` for a record a tool read — so an assistant's output never becomes the user's by
being chained. Only the record the user named is read; nothing beside it is scanned. A record
named at a later gate is read then, and its items land under their frames at the next pass.

A request that continues a field this protocol drew earlier resumes it, whether the person
took that field or left it holding: its frames, branches, candidates with their origins,
exclusions, and parks carry over as they stand in the context, together with the person's later
turns that bear on it, and the run digs on from there. The map that resumes is the whole field
— material the last map compressed to branches and counts stays reachable. A request on a new
topic starts a new run, and the earlier field stays as it stood, reachable as named material
that folds in as seeds like any other.

Chaining material in is the user's deliberate choice of substrate-first over
independent-ideation-first: that choice is their adoption of the material, recorded apart
from its origin rather than written into it.
