# `katalepsis/` — `/grasp`

**Responsibility under the Northstar** (root `AGENTS.md`). The protocol here makes sure something in play — code, a document, a result — is actually understood before work builds on it. The person says what they mean to understand, and for what purpose where they say it — in their own words, or by taking an entry point the AI offers — and their understanding is checked step by step through their own answers rather than assumed from agreement, until they say it is understood enough.

**Boundary.** `skills/grasp/SKILL.md` is the contract for `/grasp`; it names any supporting file in its `references/` to read where that file applies. `README.md`, `README_ko.md`, and `docs/` explain the protocol to people, and `.claude-plugin/` and `.codex-plugin/` carry what a host needs to list and install it; what the protocol does once invoked is set in the `SKILL.md`.
