# `katalepsis/` — `/grasp`

**Responsibility under the Northstar** (root `AGENTS.md`). The protocol here makes sure something in play — code, a document, a result — is actually understood before work builds on it. The AI first shows a map of understanding over the person's purpose — the aspects it turns on, each with what it rests on — and completes it with them one aspect at a time, checked against the target's material and the results they ask to see rather than assumed from agreement, until they say it is understood enough for their purpose.

**Boundary.** `skills/grasp/SKILL.md` is the contract for `/grasp`; it names any supporting file in its `references/` to read where that file applies. `README.md`, `README_ko.md`, and `docs/` explain the protocol to people, and `.claude-plugin/` and `.codex-plugin/` carry what a host needs to list and install it; what the protocol does once invoked is set in the `SKILL.md`.
