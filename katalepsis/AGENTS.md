# `katalepsis/` — `/grasp`

**Responsibility under the Northstar** (root `AGENTS.md`). The protocol here makes sure something in play — code, a document, a result — is actually understood before work builds on it. The AI offers entry points shaped by what the person is trying to understand, the person chooses where to start, and their understanding is checked step by step rather than assumed from agreement.

**Boundary.** `skills/grasp/SKILL.md` is the contract for `/grasp`, and the files in its `references/` are parts of that contract it points to where they apply. `README.md`, `README_ko.md`, and `docs/` explain the protocol to people, and `.claude-plugin/` and `.codex-plugin/` carry what a host needs to list and install it; what the protocol does once invoked is set in the `SKILL.md`.
