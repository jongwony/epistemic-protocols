# `hypotyposis/` — `/sketch`

**Responsibility under the Northstar** (root `AGENTS.md`). The protocol here finds a form the person cannot describe but would recognize. The AI makes concrete sketches, the person marks what does not fit and what to keep, and the AI revises until the person recognizes the form — misfit is easy to point at long before fit can be stated.

**Boundary.** `skills/sketch/SKILL.md` is the contract for `/sketch`, and the files in its `references/` are parts of that contract it points to where they apply. `README.md` and `README_ko.md` explain the protocol to people, and `.claude-plugin/` and `.codex-plugin/` carry what a host needs to list and install it; what the protocol does once invoked is set in the `SKILL.md`.
