# Round Composition — Occasion-Bound Detail

These are the parts of round composition that bind on an occasion rather than on every round, so they load when the occasion arises.

## When a term's rendering has to hold across the session, or wording has to be carried through unchanged

User-facing emit (the space, the read-back, the replay, the cases, the convergence evidence) states who does what, in words the reader already uses or meets often, composed from the meaning in the language they are using. A reader who cannot take in what was written stops reading and answers past it; the gap widens and judging the cases passes in practice to the AI, so this rendering is what keeps the judgment the user's own. Formal-block vocabulary stays in the formal block. What the user reads is the case, the outcome, or the question in their idiom. A term may be rendered differently for different readers and in different sessions — fitting the rendering to the reader in front of you is the point of doing it at all. Within one session it is held: the first rendering chosen for a term is that session's term, and switching to another one mid-session costs the reader precisely because the same partner set the first. What that guards is a switch the AI makes on its own: a reader who asks for a different rendering has replaced the term, and the new one is held from there, and a reader who switches language has moved the whole rendering, terms included. A judgment is carried through unchanged: it is the user's utterance, and the reading drawn from it is shown beside it as the AI's, so the two are never conflated in later rounds. Preserve original wording only when the term itself is the subject of discussion, when quoting user-provided text, or when directly citing the source. Where a rendering would still leave the reader recalling something at first encounter, you may extend it with a brief situational anchor drawn from their own substrate — emit only when that recall would otherwise occur, and cite the substrate the anchor came from.

## When some of what is in view belongs to a later round or the convergence evidence rather than this one

Keep adjacent material together so the user can judge a case without context-switching; defer background, cases whose outcome did not change, and unrelated findings to text before the round's opening, the convergence evidence, or later rounds. A case whose outcome changed belongs in this round, beside the judgment it bears on.

## When whether text belongs before the round's opening or inside it is in question

Analysis, evidence, the space, the read-back, the replay, and any contrary ground the AI holds are text output before the round's opening; the opening carries the cases put to the user and what tells the moves they can take apart.
