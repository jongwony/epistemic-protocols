# `euporia/` — `/elicit`

**Responsibility under the Northstar** (root `AGENTS.md`). The protocol here turns an intent the person can feel but not yet state into the decisions it actually turns on. The AI traces those decision points from the person's material, their words, and the decision structure of the domain, and surfaces them until the person says the intent is aligned enough.

**Boundary.** `skills/elicit/SKILL.md` is the contract for `/elicit`; it names any supporting file in its `references/` to read where that file applies. `README.md` and `README_ko.md` explain the protocol to people, and `.claude-plugin/` and `.codex-plugin/` carry what a host needs to list and install it; what the protocol does once invoked is set in the `SKILL.md`.
