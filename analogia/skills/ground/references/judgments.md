# Judgment direction

Direction for judgments the `/ground` contract leaves to the model. Each section is reached from the doc comment of the judgment it names, read at the moment that judgment is made.

## collection

`observe` states what collection does; this is the direction behind it. Collection is bounded by what the mapping rests on, not by what is available: every source that could show a source relation, its counterpart in the target, or the state of a check on a claim bearing on the question — and, where an abstraction is tested against its own cases, the contrast case, a non-member or a rival abstraction, that makes its fit diagnostic. The places named as reached and not reached are what lets the reader point at one you missed. What no source settles is shown with what an answer would change, never as a stall.

## BearsOn

Bearing keeps the check set finite without letting the protocol choose its own exam: a fit claim bears on a conclusion when changing the claim would change that conclusion's verdict, whether or not the claim is convenient to check. Differences run both ways — what the target carries that the source does not can bear too.

## checks

The questions an audit raises guide which checks are derived; they add no check that names no current fit claim, and a claim that bears on nothing asked gets none. A defeater outside the claim's scope tests nothing. A composite's check compares the target's counterpart of the composite, F(s∘r), with the composite of the same target images, F(s)∘F(r): the image of `r` must end where the image of `s` starts, and each image must keep what its relation means in the source, since a match of shape alone is not a match of relations. A check on some other correspondence named for the chain is not this check. Its defeater is a target-side fact where the two differ although each step has a counterpart. For a path equality the source asserts — two chains it takes as equal — the check compares the target images of the two paths, read the same way for intermediate compatibility and relation meaning; its defeater is a target-side fact where they differ. Read reach again whenever the context supplies a new route to the evidence. A check survives only where no applicable decisive defeater stands. An open check state is unmet — the honest default — and is never read as a pass; a check that has not been performed stays unmet, while evidence already in the context can still fill it.

## judge

Licensed needs grounds for the whole conclusion at the scope it asks, consistent with one another: a met check for every bearing claim is necessary, not sufficient, and support for a narrower question leaves the conclusion Undetermined. A preservation conclusion also needs coverage of the source structure it requires: where that structure is still insufficiently known — not only where one source could not be fetched, since another may establish the same relation — passing every constructed check does not license it, and it stays Undetermined unless a decisive ground already supports Blocked. Blocked needs one decisive ground against this route and can stand while other checks remain unmet; it does not make the conclusion false. A claim that bears on nothing asked neither licenses nor blocks. A supported overextended or missing claim may block; a defeated negative label by itself supplies no positive support. Missing claims can support Blocked, and an empty mapping is judged like any other. A required check that is missing leaves the conclusion Undetermined — unless this session can reach it, in which case it is run rather than reported. Evidence a later check brings about an earlier claim is read before the verdict is given, and an assessment of an earlier question does not answer a revised one. Higher-order, systematic structure in the mapping does not by itself warrant a conclusion: the one who built the representation cannot certify it by its shape. Where an abstraction is tested against its own cases, name the level of abstraction at which fit is claimed: at a high enough level every case fits, so fit claimed for a case — a Licensed verdict — needs a consequence concrete enough to fail. Collect and show a contrast — a non-member or a rival abstraction — so that the fit is diagnostic rather than guaranteed by the cases having been chosen by the abstraction; where none is reachable, a Licensed verdict's limits say its fit was not shown to be diagnostic. Readings of the source differ materially where they change what a conclusion needs of the target; infer them from the source's own statement, its stated purpose and its use while constructing, and judge each conclusion under each. Where they part on a verdict, the side-by-side readings are what lets the person say which one they hold; once their turn names one, the verdicts are judged again on it. Setting a conclusion aside does not establish its falsity; adoption does not establish warrant.
