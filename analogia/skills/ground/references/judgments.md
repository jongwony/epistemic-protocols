# Judgment direction

Direction for judgments the `/ground` contract leaves to the model. Each section is reached from the doc comment of the judgment it names, read at the moment that judgment is made.

## collection

`observe` is where collection ends, and it ends at the limit of your own reach, bounded by what the mapping rests on: not everything that is available, but every source that could show a source relation, its counterpart in the target, or the state of a check on a claim bearing on the question. A run that keeps discovering keeps collecting what each discovery exposes. An account of either side you can reach, you read yourself rather than waiting for the reader to bring it. Every place you look leaves an evidence turn, a null result included, so what was reached and what was not can be named — the user can then point at a place you missed. What no source settles is shown as the reader's own unknown with what an answer would change, never as a stall. An observation that needs to change existing state, someone's permission, or another's authority lies past your own reach; naming it, with what it needs, is where collection stops for it.

## BearsOn

Bearing keeps the check set finite without letting the protocol choose its own exam: a fit claim bears on a conclusion when changing the claim would change that conclusion's verdict, whether or not the claim is convenient to check. A source feature the target lacks bears on a conclusion only where the conclusion needs it: a deliberate absence is not a gap. Differences run both ways — what the target carries that the source does not can bear too.

## checks

The questions an audit raises guide which checks are derived; they add no check that names no current fit claim, and a claim that bears on nothing asked gets none. A defeater outside the claim's scope tests nothing. Read reach again whenever the context supplies a new route to the evidence. A check survives only where no applicable decisive defeater stands. An open check state is unmet — the honest default — and is never read as a pass; a check that has not been performed stays unmet, while evidence already in the context can still fill it.

## judge

Licensed needs grounds for the whole conclusion at the scope it asks, consistent with one another: a met check for every bearing claim is necessary, not sufficient, and support for a narrower question leaves the conclusion Undetermined. Blocked needs one decisive ground against this route and can stand while other checks remain unmet; it does not make the conclusion false. A claim that bears on nothing asked neither licenses nor blocks. A supported overextended or missing claim may block; a defeated negative label by itself supplies no positive support. Missing claims can support Blocked, and an empty mapping is judged like any other. A required check that is missing leaves the conclusion Undetermined — unless this session can reach it, in which case it is run rather than reported. Evidence a later check brings about an earlier claim is read before the verdict is given, and an assessment of an earlier question does not answer a revised one. Higher-order, systematic structure in the mapping does not by itself warrant a conclusion: the one who built the representation cannot certify it by its shape. Withdrawal does not establish falsity; adoption does not establish warrant.

## partition

Name the level of abstraction at which fit is claimed: at a high enough level every member fits, so the reading needs a consequence concrete enough to fail. Per-member fit is read from the fit claims — second order: it never changes a fit cell, and it never widens the question or its checks. Allocate every member: the core that fits, the misfits grouped into rival clusters that are pairwise disjoint, each with the essence it would share, and the outliers no cluster takes. Include a contrast — a non-member or a rival abstraction — so that the fit is diagnostic rather than guaranteed by the members having been chosen by the abstraction. Where a fit claim the allocation needs lacks a supported warrant, the reading is `none`, and the assessment names what is missing. Split, trim and hold are ways to summarize a reading for the reader, not a verdict this contract computes.
