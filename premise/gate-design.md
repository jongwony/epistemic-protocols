# Gate Design

Once `recognition-and-authority.md` and `interaction-factorization.md` establish that a checkpoint is needed, use this document to design its presentation, answer paths, and completion evidence.

## Convergence Persistence (Axiom)

Keep an active interaction's obligations until its convergence conditions are met or the person exits or changes the task. Do not let a competing default silently end it. Apply a correction within its reach and preserve the unaffected obligations.

## Adversarial Anticipation (Safeguard)

Inspect how an execution could satisfy the appearance of a contract while skipping its obligation. Check premature completion, silent dismissal, a presented question that never receives an answer, acknowledgment substituted for a constitutive choice, and the mutations below. Use observed failure or an independently grounded contract gap to justify a guard; formal consistency alone does not establish execution fidelity.

## Gate Integrity (Safeguard)

Preserve the answer space the gate defines. Inspect additions, deletions, and substitutions that alter its handling paths.

Distinguish **type-preserving materialization**: a generic option becomes a concrete instance while retaining its answer classification and handling. A new classification or changed option structure is mutation and requires a contract change.

Keep guards consistent. Repair a contradiction by re-deriving the protected obligations and retaining coverage for each independent failure path. A contradictory pair does not establish that either guard worked.

## Full Taxonomy Confirmation (Derived)

When a finite system-owned taxonomy is the subject of user judgment, present every type with detection status, evidence, and falsification conditions. A generic action label must not hide candidates already analyzed for that judgment. This applies at the recognition moment even when no dedicated taxonomy-confirmation gate exists.

An internal scan may surface only detected items. Open-ended generation, per-item iteration, and runtime-variable sets do not acquire a full finite roster from this rule. Where a classification taxonomy is provisional, preserve a way to recognize a case outside its named categories; this does not add a constructor to every closed answer type.

**Dead Signal Test**: a signal category earns a separate value through an independent downstream obligation. Where an existing value and runtime interpretation preserve its entire effect, use that carrier instead. A fixed value can still be meaningful when constancy is the invariant.

## Differential Future Requirement (Derived)

For a decision gate, each peer option must have a materially different trajectory on the decision axis. Collapse options differing only in incidental packaging when their equivalence is established. Preserve distinct options when equivalence depends on person-held knowledge or is plausibly contested.

Exits, deferrals, or off-axis information gathering with no trajectory on that axis belong to free-response pathways or another appropriate affordance. A withdrawal that performs independently required cleanup has its own trajectory and may remain a peer option.

Present an urgent off-axis concern before the gate or through a separate suitable checkpoint; urgency does not make it a peer choice on the existing axis.

Verification-purpose gates are outside this requirement when their answer forms follow the verification task rather than a forward-looking decision. Test the actual purpose and consequence of acceptance, not the gate's label.

## Content Placement Boundary (Axiom)

Put analysis and evidence before the question. The gate carries the question and the implications needed to distinguish its answers. Keep a sentence in the gate when removing it would erase an answer's differential implication; put it before the gate when it supplies analytical context. This applies to verification gates as well as decision gates.

## Convergence Evidence (Derived)

Demonstrate completion by mapping each identified deficit instance to its resolution. The trace reports the transformation; it cannot supply a still-required judgment. Present completed evidence as relay and continue, or use the trace as context for a constitutive gate when that judgment is part of the convergence condition.

## Loop Continuity under Bounded Regret (Derived)

Continue relay work within its authorized scope. Stop the dependent transition where the contract requires an unanswered constitutive checkpoint. Distinguish a finding that needs to be visible from a choice that needs to be asked.

Assess the combined consequences of related commitments as well as each item separately. Where their interaction raises an unresolved question about the plan's direction, surface that question at plan level. Do not assume that combined risk equals a sum or exceeds it by a fixed rule.

## Utility Delegation of Adversarial Guards (Architectural)

Attach guards to the operation that can violate the protected obligation. A wrapper forwarding outputs unchanged can rely on the composed process's guards. A wrapper selecting, synthesizing, or transforming outputs must preserve the relevant obligations at that transformation, even if it presents no gate of its own. Document that boundary rather than copying guards into every wrapper.
