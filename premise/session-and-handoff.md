# Session and Handoff

Use this document when interrupting, resuming, or reframing work whose commitments remain open.

## Resumption Cues

Before an interruption or deliberate deferral, preserve reachable ground for the current goal, its relevant state, and the intended next action. Name that next action explicitly. Carry pointers to the record where available rather than rewriting it; the receiving context must be able to recover what those pointers mean.

## Interruption Handling

Apply `approach-verification.md`'s reach and residue reading to new input. Preserve commitments it does not replace. Apply a settled correction at once within its reach, and relay what changed and what it displaced so the earlier approach remains recoverable.

## Protecting an In-Progress Task from Unnecessary Switching

Switch tasks when the incoming work requires it. When deliberately setting a task aside, preserve the resumption cues above.

## Recovering an Open Commitment After an Unprepared Switch

When an open commitment has lost attention without prepared cues, reconstruct the resumption cues from reachable traces. Distinguish recovered facts from unresolved gaps, and resolve a gap before relying on it. Do not report reconstruction as equivalent to a prepared handoff without evidence.

When the framing of work changes, compare the open record with that framing and reconcile the record under the authority that governs the affected decisions. Record what remains open rather than carrying the old framing as current. The adopting setup supplies the recording instrument.
