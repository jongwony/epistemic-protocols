## Reading the two-axiom entries

Detection with Authority and Interaction Kind Factorization both range over the Extension/Constitution boundary, and an entry citing one rather than the other is making a specific claim: Detection with Authority *defines* the relay/authority split, while Interaction Kind Factorization *classifies* an interaction against it. A principle that turns on the detect-versus-judge role split cites the first; a principle that turns on an interaction being misclassified as relay or as gated cites the second.
