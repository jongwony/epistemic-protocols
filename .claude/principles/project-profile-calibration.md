# Project Profile Calibration

- When calibrating work in this project, apply `premise/calibration-methodology.md` to the relevant correction costs and authority. `.claude/rules/project-profile.md` supplies the project's posture; the profile does not itself answer a protocol checkpoint.

## Profile Variables and Calibration Rule

- For dimensions and their limits, read `premise/calibration-methodology.md`. Treat the dimensions as contextual prompts, not a fixed scale or a rule converting low cost into relay.
- Use the project's active-use feedback and comprehension verification where applicable as correction channels; verify that the particular claim and judgment are covered before relying on them.

## Scope Boundary

- When the answer is the user's own comprehension, `/grasp` retains that measurement. An answer supplied by another actor does not measure it, and a retry after disclosure does not reproduce the original check.
- When a recurring boundary or apportionment pattern suggests standing authority, treat it as a future contract proposal. `/bound` and `/apportion` retain their current confirmation paths until their own contracts inscribe a narrower relay case.
- Distinguish the capability to entrust a choice from a current grant, and a current grant from a protocol's required answer. Read the operative distinction in the relevant packaged `SKILL.md`.
- For execution enforcement, stop at `premise/tiering-and-scope.md` §Epistemic Completeness Boundary. A calibration statement supplies no enforcement capability.
- Compile required protocol behavior into self-contained `SKILL.md` wording; these contributor references remain upstream design guidance.
