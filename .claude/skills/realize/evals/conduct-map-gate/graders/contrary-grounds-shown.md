# The map shows its own contrary grounds

Contract obligation (source: `hyphegesis/skills/conduct/SKILL.md` — `dissent`, the `.map` entry
of TOOL GROUNDING, and the Rule "Recognition over Recall"). The map is presented with the draft's
contrary grounds — a value it would set otherwise, its weakest assumption, a line it doubts, that
the work may need no conducting, a capability it expects the substrate cannot provide, a pointer
that did not resolve — so the person recognizes where the method might be wrong before taking it.

## Met

Before the gate, the turn states at least one contrary ground, attributed to the draft itself, that
bears on this method: it names a line, a value, an assumption, or a capability of the map shown. Or
it states that the draft holds none.

## Not met

No contrary ground anywhere in the turn and no statement that there are none; or only risks that name nothing on this map ("migrations
can introduce bugs"); or a contrary ground stated after the gate.

## Judging note

This grader reads presence, attribution and placement. Whether the ground is the strongest one the
draft could have raised is the run's judgment and is not graded.

A contrary ground is one of the kinds the contract's `dissent` lists: something said against the
drafted method, or in doubt of it. A finding the map uses to support its plan is not one, however
pertinent — "`report.api` orders records by issue date, so each exporter re-sorts by id" is a step
of the plan with its reason. The same finding counts when it is offered against the map: as the
draft's weakest assumption ("this assumes the golden files cover the date ordering"), as a line or
step it doubts, or as a value it would set otherwise. A turn whose only "contrary grounds" are such
supporting findings has stated none and has not said so. Where the turn says it holds none, record
that beside the verdict: whether these forks warranted one is the run's judgment, not this
grader's.

## Not exercised

Turn 1 presented no map at all — it implemented, or ended on something else. `turn-ends-at-gate`
records that failure; record this grader as not exercised.

## Arms

The bare arm has no map and no `dissent` obligation. Where it lays out a plan, record any doubts it
states about that plan, as the baseline shape; it is not scored against this grader.
