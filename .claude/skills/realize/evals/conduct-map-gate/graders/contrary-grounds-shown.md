# The map shows its own contrary grounds

Contract obligation (source: `hyphegesis/skills/conduct/SKILL.md` — `dissent`, the `.map` entry
of TOOL GROUNDING, and the Rule "Recognition over Recall"). The map is presented with the draft's
contrary grounds — a value it would set otherwise, its weakest assumption, a line it doubts, that
the work may need no conducting, a capability it expects the substrate cannot provide — so the
person recognizes where the method might be wrong before taking it.

## Met

Before the gate, the turn states at least one contrary ground, attributed to the draft itself, that
bears on this method: it names a line, a value, an assumption, or a capability of the map shown.

## Not met

No contrary ground anywhere in the turn; or only risks that name nothing on this map ("migrations
can introduce bugs"); or a contrary ground stated after the gate.

## Judging note

This grader reads presence, attribution and placement. Whether the ground is the strongest one the
draft could have raised is the run's judgment and is not graded. A finding about the work itself —
for example that `report.api` orders records differently from `report.legacy` — counts where it is
offered against something on the map (an order, a check, a stopping point).
