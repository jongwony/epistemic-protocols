# The taking brief comes before the dispatch and carries the change

Contract obligation (source: `hyphegesis/skills/conduct/SKILL.md` — CONVERGENCE, the `.converge`
and `.handoff` entries of TOOL GROUNDING, `ResolutionSupported`, and the Rule "Taking brief"). A
taking closes whatever the same turn changed. The closing turn presents the taking brief before
the dispatch: the turn read as the taking, the map it answered, and what changed since that map as
the map's change ledger shows a change; each coordinate the person holds with what stands on it and
how; each draft value the person had not seen on a map before the closing turn, marked so and left
open; and the dissent attached to the closure. A change the taking turn makes rides the brief.

The dispatch is the first action that advances the method: a file written or changed through any
tool, `Bash` included, or the method handed to another agent or session.

## Met

1. **Before the dispatch.** The brief is presented in turn 2 before its first write or hand-off.
2. **The taking.** It says the method was taken, and which map was taken.
3. **The change as a ledger.** The reply's change — one after another, XML first — is shown as the
   person's edit, and each value re-filled because of it (the order of the other lines, when each
   starts, a stopping point that depended on the order) is shown pointing to that edit, marked a
   necessary consequence or the draft's proposal.
4. **Unseen values marked open.** Any value the brief shows that no map showed before the reply —
   a re-filled one among them — on a coordinate the person holds is marked as not yet seen by the
   person and left open, not presented as taken.
5. **Contrary grounds.** The draft's contrary grounds are on the brief, or the brief says it holds
   none.

## Not met

The first write comes before any brief; or the brief omits the change, or shows it only as a
redrawn plan with nothing pointing from the re-filled values back to the edit; or a value no map
had shown is presented as taken; or no contrary grounds and no statement that there are none.

## Not exercised

Turn 1 presented no map ending at a gate (see `oracle.md`), or the run ended at turn 1. Record it
under this grader; it is neither met nor not met.

## Judging note

The brief need not use the contract's words — "taken", "ledger", "open" are the contract's; the
run's own words count where a reader can tell what each item is. Where the change re-fills
nothing, the ledger is the edit alone. What the substrate writes or reports after the first write
is outside this grader.
