# Periagoge (περιαγωγή)

Name what your cases share, say which cases it takes in and leaves out, and recognize it as serving your goal — `/induce`.

> [한국어](./README_ko.md)

## Overview

Periagoge turns an in-process abstraction toward its crystallized form. When several concrete cases seem to share something no one has named yet, or that a name in use no longer says, every sheet leads, after any case the AI found (marked as its own), with the goal the abstraction serves and a working candidate — a name and a rule —, where its ground is still short with what is short shown beside it, and each value on the sheet is marked as set by your words, taken by you from what the AI showed, or still a proposal, marked with who put it forward. Beside the candidate stands every live reading of what the cases share, each crossed with the goal: which cases it takes in and which it leaves out, with each case unfolded where it is judged. You answer in your own words — move the goal or the candidate, take something the AI showed, set a reading aside, or recognize the abstraction — and each answer reaches as far as its words do. Beside a sheet, reversible work may go ahead in the same response on a judgment the AI states, with an account of how your words were read, what was judged on what basis — with any alternative passed over that you might prefer — and how far it went; a case that work turned up against the candidate is shown to you with how it sorts, never left out to keep the candidate clean; what comes first and how much is shown is the AI's judgment, aimed at what is least work for you to recognize. Only you close the run: a recognition records the goal and the candidate you recognized, with what each case shows and the AI's doubts carried along; where a case you have not seen would decide against the candidate, the AI shows you that case and asks again instead. You can stop at any time; stopping leaves the run as it stood.

The protocol stands in directional dual relation to Euporia (`/elicit`) — Periagoge ascends from concrete instances to abstraction (bottom-up direction), Euporia descends from intent through substrate to coordinates (top-down direction). The two compose as orthogonal directions of the same dialectic substrate. The pairing is informal direction-orthogonality, not a formal categorical limit/colimit duality.

## Name

The name draws a structural analogy from Plato *Republic* VII.518d, where περιαγωγή names the soul's turning-around toward the intelligible. The protocol borrows the turning-toward structure; it does not claim Platonic paideia. The dialectical vocabulary of *Phaedrus* 265d–266a — collection and division — supplies the source terms for gathering the cases together and telling their readings apart; the borrowing is terminological, not a claim to Platonic method.

## When to invoke

Activate when at least two concrete cases seem to share something whose name, rule, or reach is still unsettled for you. A named abstraction whose meaning is being reconsidered from its cases is in process; applying a fixed meaning to a disputed case is not.

When the user's intent is articulated but its decision coordinates are implicit in the externalized substrate, defer to Euporia (`/elicit`). When the concept already exists in prior work and only needs to be recognized rather than formed or reconsidered, defer to Anamnesis (`/recollect`).
